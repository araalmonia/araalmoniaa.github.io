Project Two

Project Two involves developing a website for our university to provide students, faculty, staff, and visitors with an accessible and convenient platform for obtaining important academic information. The main problem it addresses is the need for a centralized online space where users can easily access university announcements, academic programs, campus updates, events, and other essential resources. Our solution is to create a user-friendly, responsive, and visually appealing website that organizes information clearly and allows users to navigate different sections efficiently. The website is designed to improve communication between the university and its community while making important information more accessible across different devices. Through this project, I aim to contribute to the university’s digital presence by creating a functional and reliable platform that supports communication, accessibility, and the needs of the academic community.
